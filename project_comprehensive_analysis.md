# HireMintora: AI-Driven Autonomous Recruitment & Assessment System
## Comprehensive Technical Analysis & Project Documentation

### 1. Motivation & Problem Statement
The recruitment landscape currently faces significant bottlenecks that HireMintora aims to solve:
*   **Application Overload**: HR departments are overwhelmed by massive volumes of resumes, leading to manual oversight and missed talent.
*   **Unconscious Bias**: Human recruiters often suffer from subjective biases during resume parsing and initial screening.
*   **Scheduling Inefficiency**: The "email tag" loop for interview scheduling causes significant delays in the hiring pipeline.
*   **Resource Drain**: Initial technical and behavioral screenings are repetitive and consume hundreds of hours of high-value engineering/HR time.
*   **Fragmented Feedback**: Post-interview evaluations are often unstructured, leading to slow and subjective decision-making.

**Solution**: An end-to-end autonomous system that parses resumes, ranks candidates, conducts real-time adaptive AI interviews (with proctoring), and generates data-driven executive reports.

### 2. Implementation Overview
HireMintora is built on a modern, scalable microservices-inspired architecture:

*   **Backend Framework**: FastAPI (Python 3.10+) for high-performance asynchronous execution.
*   **Database**: SQLAlchemy ORM with support for PostgreSQL/SQLite for robust state management.
*   **Real-time Interaction**: WebSockets (via `ai_interview_bot/router.py`) for low-latency candidate-bot communication.
*   **AI Orchestration**:
    *   **Logic**: OpenAI GPT-4o-mini for adaptive conversational intelligence.
    *   **Voice**: Sarvam TTS/STT for high-quality audio interaction.
    *   **Synthesis**: Groq (LLM) for ultra-fast generation of executive candidate summaries.
*   **Frontend**: React & TypeScript with Monaco Editor integration for live coding assessments.

### 3. Core Algorithms & Deep Learning Engine
The "DL Engine" is the crown jewel of the system, providing a three-tiered assessment model:

#### A. Multi-Task DeBERTa Scorer
*   **Base Model**: `microsoft/deberta-v3-small`.
*   **Architecture**: Fine-tuned with **8 specialized regression heads** and 1 classification head.
*   **Metrics Tracked**:
    *   Initiative, Accountability, Ownership, Reliability
    *   Ethical Decision Making, Team Impact, Maturity, Role Readiness
*   **Uncertainty Quantificaton**: Implements **Monte Carlo (MC) Dropout** to generate 95% confidence intervals for every score, ensuring reliability in evaluation.

#### B. Behavioral BiLSTM
*   **Purpose**: Analyzes the "integrity" of the candidate's session.
*   **Mechanism**: Processes sequential event data from proctoring violations (e.g., tab switching, face detection alerts) to output a temporal **Authenticity Score**.

#### C. Multimodal Fusion Network
*   **Operation**: Uses a fusion layer to weight contributions from three modalities:
    *   **Text (70%)**: Deep semantic analysis of interview transcripts.
    *   **Behavioral (20%)**: Temporal patterns from the BiLSTM proctoring analysis.
    *   **Task (10%)**: Quantitative performance on MCQ and DSA assessments.

### 5. Project Results & Outcomes
1.  **Automated Executive Reports**: Generates a professional 2-page PDF summarizing the candidate’s technical, behavioral, and authenticity profile within seconds of interview completion.
2.  **Adaptive Interviewing**: The bot dynamically adjusts its flow—asking follow-ups for vague answers and skipping topics once proficiency is demonstrated.
3.  **Bias Reduction**: Decisions are based on fine-tuned DL models and predefined scoring rubrics rather than recruiter intuition.
4.  **Integrity Assurance**: Real-time proctoring coupled with the BiLSTM authenticity model ensures that results are representative of the candidate’s true abilities.
5.  **Operational Efficiency**: Reduces the time-to-hire by automating the most resource-intensive stages of the recruitment funnel.
